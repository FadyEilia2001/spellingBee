# Spelling study

A practice app where a child spells words from a fixed study list, either in a sized round, on a single page, or as a word pick from one page.

## Language

**Difficulty**:
A length band chosen for a round: Short (3–5 letters), Medium (6–8 letters), or Long (9 or more letters). Each difficulty contains only words longer than the one before it.
_Avoid_: tier, level, One Bee

**Page**:
One of the six study lists, in list order: Page 1 through Page 6.
_Avoid_: difficulty, sheet, screen, One Bee

**Quiz**:
The adventure that starts a round. She picks a difficulty, then how many words.
_Avoid_: practice a round

**Round**:
A practice session of a chosen number of different words from one difficulty. A word that is not spelled cleanly comes back later, so the round can last more turns than the number chosen.
_Avoid_: session, deck

**Page lesson**:
A study of one whole page, in that page's order, which continues until every word on the page has a clean spelling.
_Avoid_: round

**Word pick**:
A lesson of the words she highlighted on one page, kept in that page's order, which continues until every highlighted word has a clean spelling.
_Avoid_: custom quiz, partial page, trunk

**Clean spelling**:
Spelling the word correctly on the first try of its current turn.
_Avoid_: mastered, solved

**Set**:
Ten words of a page in page order (Words 1–10, 11–20, …), studied as a page lesson.
_Avoid_: chunk, batch

**Bee mode**:
A round from one school level (One, Two or Three Bee) played like the stage: one try, a minute on the clock, no taking letters back, and asks for definition, sentence, part of speech and origin.
_Avoid_: hard mode, contest

**Learned word**:
A word she has spelled cleanly at least once, or correctly in Bee mode.
_Avoid_: mastered

**Practice words**:
Words she missed that are not learned yet.
_Avoid_: mistakes, wrong words

**Keep going**:
Picking up a paused lesson, round or Bee mode where she left off. A practice run is never paused this way.
_Avoid_: resume, continue
